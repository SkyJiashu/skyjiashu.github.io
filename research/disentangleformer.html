Spatial-Channel Decoupling for Multi-Channel Vision

Jiashu Liao, Pietro Liò, Marc de Kamps, Duygu Sarikaya

arXiv v1 · First authorTransformers · Hyperspectral imaging · Representations

Separate spatial and channel modelling, then fuse the complementary streams. The architecture combines parallel Transformer pathways, the Squeezed Token Enhancer (STE), and a Multi-Scale Feed-Forward Network (MS-FFN).
