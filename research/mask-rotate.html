Mask Rotate / RMAGAN

Self-supervised random mask attention GAN in tackling pose-invariant face recognition

Jiashu Liao, Tanaya Guha, Victor Sanchez

Published · First & corresponding authorSelf-supervision · Face completion · Generative vision

The Mask Rotate framework learns to complete missing facial texture without paired frontal-view supervision. RMAGAN combines a Siamese encoder with landmark-guided attention; rendering and DifFace post-processing are used during inference, not as the training-time rotation pipeline.

FrameworkNetworkVisual resultsRotation & limitations
01

Framework

Training and inference are different. Random Mask simulates missing texture during training, removing the need for a neural rendering step in that stage. At inference, a pre-trained renderer produces the rotated input for completion.

02

Network

RMAGAN uses a Siamese landmark-guided attention-selective generator. The flipped input supplies complementary spatial information, while landmark features guide fusion.

03

Visual results

The paper evaluates the method on LFW and IJB-A and includes CelebA-HQ for visual comparison. The examples below retain the original comparison labels and show the distinction between the completion model and the added DifFace post-processing.

04

Rotation & limitations

Large changes in viewpoint expose unseen texture. The paper explicitly reports reduced performance for rotations that differ by 90° or more from the input pose, and limitations inherited from the pre-trained renderer.
